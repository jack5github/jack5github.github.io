MECCHA CHAMELEON is an online multiplayer game most similar to [Garry's Mod](/article/gmod) Hide & Seek, with the standout difference being that all players are poseable featureless characters that can be painted to blend in with the environment. It has a number of gamemodes and supports custom maps through its [Steam Workshop](https://steamcommunity.com/workshop/browse?appid=4704690). It uses [Epic Online Services](https://onlineservices.epicgames.com/) for its peer-to-peer multiplayer, and a large amount of packets are sent between players, leading to network instability, therefore this game can only be played when no-one else in a household is using the Internet for anything else intensive.

## Map configurations

- **Increasing Tag**
  - *Number of Hunters* - 1
  - *Hunter Wait Time (sec)* - 85
  - *Search Time (sec)* - 300
  - *Answer Check Time (sec)* - 40
  - *Forced Taunt Interval (sec)* - 30
  - *Ammo Limit* - Off
  - *Show Missed-Spot Ranking to Hunters* - On
- **Reverse Chicken Race**
  - *Paint Time (sec)* - 120
  - *Observation Time (sec)* - 15
  - *Mid-Game Search Time (sec)* - 120
  - *Mid-Game Answer Check Time (sec)* - 20
  - *Answer Check Time (sec)* - 20
  - *Forced Taunt Interval (sec)* - 45
  - *Ammo Limit* - Off
  - *Show Missed-Spot Ranking to Hunters* - On
