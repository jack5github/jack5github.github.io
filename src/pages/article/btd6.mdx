Bloons TD 6 is a top-down tower defense game with online co-op multiplayer for up to 4 players. Players place monkeys on various maps in order to stop balloons of differing strengths and resistances from escaping. Each subsequent wave of balloons is more difficult to defend against.

## Unlock Small Monkey Towers

The [Small Monkey Towers](https://bloons.fandom.com/wiki/Small_Monkey_Towers_Option) option reduces the size of all monkeys on the map, making it easier to see what is going on and how much space is left to deploy monkeys in. It is unlocked by earning the achievement **Golden Ticket** on the Beginner map **Candy Falls**. Watch the below video for where and how to click on the map in order to get the achievement.
