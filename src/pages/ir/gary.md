Gary B. Gone (middle name unknown) was **Glitch_Out_Of_This_World**'s player character in the **interp'd Reality** campaign up until the end of [episode 23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=H9lY2VeudXM).

Gary B. Gone is the son of a noble family of elves with a Ranger build, and was the eldest out of his party members, yet not the tallest (second to [Hmm](/ir/hmm)). He is a light skinned fellow with dull green eyes and stylised black-blueish hair. He wears an old-timey duller-green tunic with long dull blue sleeves and a green cape, plus brown pants and puttees with green diagonal straps. Despite his elf heritage, he appears more humanoid than most, with the only true differentiator being his pointed ears.

Before finding himself in the WasteNet, Gary hailed from the land of **Bhaw**, although his time there was not particularly pleasant, getting emotional whiplash from losing his parents and being outclassed by his sisters **Mina** and **Lina**. The one thing that followed him from Bhaw was the one-sided relationship between himself and Hmm, as despite being bullied by the anthropomorphic dragon's friend **Igon**, he would go out searching for Hmm when he went missing one day. After being sent from dimension to dimension against his will (especially **The Great Forest** where he acquired an immense hatred for flora), he finally wound up in the **WasteNet** where he reunited with Hmm, and also met a number of other adventurers in roughly the same boat as him. Gary proved to be the one in his party to always have an alternative perspective to the others, pulling them away should they become too distracted with his own distractions. He was also able to follow through on any vendettas he acquired with incredible dedication and ease. Due to his selfishness when using **Lieu** objects, he began to be haunted by the sleep paralysis demon **Floro**, but eventually Floro turned good and afterwards revealed visions to Gary that were critical to his mission.

Gary was removed prematurely from the world of the WasteNet, due to a glitch on his shoulder that was the result of **Cuddlemaw** attacking him, and horribly timed too, as this happened moments before an unscheduled server restart. Gary would end up in a different dimension, which reminded him of but was not his hometown. He would be joined by **Adefire Wynness**, and together have gone on a new adventure.
